Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 11, 2026

Equalizer Bass Booster - Notch ("Notch" or "the App") is an equalizer and bass booster for Android, published by Lowbit Labs. The package name is com.lowbit.notch. This policy explains how the App handles your data.

Data Collection

Notch does not require an account, and Lowbit Labs does not include its own analytics SDK. Audio processing happens entirely on your device. The App does not record audio and does not upload audio.

The App does show banner ads through the Google AdMob SDK. AdMob collects data and shares it with Google, as described under Advertising. Lowbit Labs does not sell your data.

Audio

Equalizer and bass processing run on your device. Audio you play is not recorded by the App and is not sent to Lowbit Labs or to any server we operate.

Advertising

Banner ads are provided by Google AdMob. AdMob collects the following and shares it with Google:

  • Approximate location, derived from your IP address
  • App interactions
  • Crash logs and diagnostics
  • Device or other IDs, including the Android advertising ID

Google uses this information for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention and security.

For more detail, read Google's privacy policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Payments

A one-time in-app purchase removes ads. The purchase is processed by Google Play billing. We do not receive your payment details.

Security

Data collected through the App is encrypted in transit.

Your choices

You can reset or delete your Android advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device settings. Menu names vary by Android version. Ads may still appear after you change these settings. Deleting the advertising ID means later ads are not based on that ID, though they can still be based on other factors. Resetting it replaces the ID, and personalized ads can continue under the new one.

Delete your advertising ID

  1. Open the Settings app.
  2. Tap Privacy, then Ads. On some devices, open Settings, tap Google, then All services, and under Privacy & security tap Ads.
  3. Tap Delete advertising ID and confirm.

Deleting the advertising ID removes it and does not assign a new one.

Reset your advertising ID

  1. Open the Settings app.
  2. Tap Privacy, then Ads.
  3. Tap Reset advertising ID and confirm.

Resetting replaces the current ID with a new one.

Opt out of personalized ads

  • On many current devices: open Settings, tap Google, then All services, and under Privacy & security tap Ads. Use the controls there to opt out of personalized advertising.
  • On some older versions of Android: open Settings, tap Privacy, then Advanced, then Ads, and turn on Opt out of Ads Personalization.

Children

The App is not directed to children under 13.
